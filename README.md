The reclist was created to be complete, with more phonemes and easy to record for native and foreign people, the reclist includes extra phonemes, but its negative point is the probable difficulty to record some consonants, considering that the reclist has several of these besides being extensive compared to other cvvc reclists
its organization is based on the IPE-A reclist for PT-BR, with permission from the creator (Tiffany)
terms of use:
can record voicebanks without needing to specify which reclist (however credits are admired)
redistribution is allowed however give the proper credits or leave the name "kiyuhua" (creator) in the file
it is allowed to edit the reclist for own use, editing and distributing is allowed but credits are extremely necessary, it is not allowed to claim ownership of the reclist.

any doubt about the terms of use, or need to report errors in the reclist, contact the creator on discord: xing.kiyuhua

the reclist is strongly based on romaji and the symbols that are being used to indicate pronunciation of the connotation are from IPA/international phonetic alphabet
